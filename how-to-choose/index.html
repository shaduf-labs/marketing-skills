Five-job chooser

Draft a marketing page

Choose: Copywriting. Use it for a reviewable page draft from authorized context.

Do not choose: when you need a plan, web research, an audit, campaign analysis, or direct publication.

Plan content work

Choose: Content Strategy. Use it for pillars, priorities, and an editorial calendar when public-web research and authorized business inputs are acceptable.

Do not choose: when external access is not allowed or the work must be executed immediately.

Find competitors

Choose: Competitor Discovery. Use it for a public-web starting set that a person will confirm.

Do not choose: when web fetches are not allowed or current claims will not be reviewed.

Audit a page

Choose: Search Page Audit. Use it for an authorized public URL and prioritized fixes.

Do not choose: for a protected URL without authorization or a task that requires automatic deployment.

Review campaign metrics

Choose: Ad Campaign Analyzer. Use it for an authorized export when a qualified person can review any budget decision.

Do not choose: when data cannot be shared safely or account operation is expected.
