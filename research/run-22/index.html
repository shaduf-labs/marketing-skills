Research report · 12 October 2026 · Run 22

Copywriting can structure a draft; it cannot approve a claim

Retain and narrow the Copywriting card for one owner-approved local landing-page draft. Start with an owner-maintained brief/template and reviewed prompt. The source provides a page-copy scaffold, not verified conversion effects, an AI detector, installation clearance or publication authority.

Decision boundary

A named owner maps each material claim, proof block and exact CTA destination to approved evidence or holds it. Legal/compliance and site reviewers check their respective concerns. Publication is a separate decision after rendered-page review. No route was executed or compared.

Pool-proposed claim-to-proof contract—not enforced by the skill

For one approved, non-sensitive local brief, record one row per material proposed claim: exact draft wording and section; owner-supplied fact or dated source; evidence owner and use rights; audience, product and market scope; numeric calculation, denominator and qualifiers; status approved / revise / hold; named reviewer and decision date. Keep a separate list of missing or contradictory facts. This is our review template, not a source requirement.

1 · Brief gateHold draft handoff if page goal, audience, offer, traffic origin or one CTA is unknown or contradictory in a way that changes the promise. Do not read unapproved local product context.
2 · Claim gateHold or omit unsupported numbers, rates, time savings, “best/first” and causal outcomes. Do not reuse the skill's efficacy figures or illustrative metrics. Keep necessary qualifiers.
3 · Rights and terms gateHold testimonials, logos and case results without exact source, consent/use rights and qualifiers; comparisons without matched dated evidence; guarantees, refunds, security or compliance promises without approved terms.
4 · Destination and release gateVerify the exact CTA URL, destination, action and terms. Label placeholders rather than make them clickable release copy. Marketing checks facts and voice; specialists review regulated claims; the site owner checks the rendered page. Separately authorize publication or CMS connection.

A missing claim may become a narrower verified fact or a visible [NEED: proof/terms] hold, not a confident paraphrase.

This preference is a process inference, not a head-to-head result. A consented same-brief comparison with a fixed claim/proof rubric could test whether the skill prevents recurring omissions beyond the template. No such comparison exists.

Limits and reconsideration

The run inspected current returned selected text, three directly needed references and three cited research abstracts. The mutable views were marked crawled today, not pinned. It did not establish a full present inventory, reference-loading behavior, authorized-data scope, host behavior or safety. No real brief, customer facts, page, CMS, conversion data or host was supplied; no skill or prompt was installed, run, quality-compared or tested for AI detection or conversion.

Reconsider or remove the card if a verified full revision requires unauthorized reads or actions, or if missing proof repeatedly becomes confident promises. Documented skill-only improvements across consented cases could justify a controlled later evaluation—not immediate installation or publication. Stop before install advice until the intended revision, full resources and host/data scope are reviewed; stop before external release until named reviewers approve it.
