Research report · 7 October 2026 · Run 17

Ad Campaign Analyzer leaves the active chooser

The selected source instructs immediate stop/scale decisions from export metrics. Its profitability and significance labels are not supported by the inputs it requires. This is an original-source decision audit, not a package or campaign test.

Decision: no paid-media skill recommendation here

Remove Ad Campaign Analyzer from the five active source-inspected candidates. Keep its dated record for historical comparison. Do not install it, use its Red/Yellow/Green grades as spend advice, or change an account from its output. For descriptive export triage, use an authorized export in a governed worksheet; a named paid-media owner must validate definitions, lag, attribution and economics, then separately approve any account or spend change.

What was actually checked

The mutable selected SKILL.md was obtained on 7 October, displayed version 1.0 and was returned as crawled today. The selected-directory listing was about five months old and cannot establish current inventory. The README was returned as crawled today and the license as crawled last week. Selected-file SHA/date, tag attribution and current package contents remain unknown. This reading does not clear Run 5’s stop before installation advice.

Source inputExport or pasted table, target CPA and AOV. Date range, conversion definition/value, currency, attribution window, lag and non-media costs are not required.
Source outputTraffic-light grade, “Immediate (Today)” stop/scale list and a 20%-every-48-hours budget plan. No direct account API or credential use is stated.
Pool decisionDo not route users to that action plan. A governed worksheet can recompute descriptive metrics and expose unknowns; an authorized owner separately decides action.

What to do instead—and what could change this decision

For a permitted export, record source, period, metric definitions, denominators and missing fields in a governed spreadsheet or analyst worksheet. Recompute descriptive CTR and CPA; mark conversion lag, attribution, margin/LTV and reconciliation unknown where they are unknown. Stop before inferring profit, causality or a budget move. An authorized specialist reviews any subsequent decision.

Reconsider a card only if a fully enumerated, attributable selected revision removes the unsupported profit/significance/20%-rule claims, or makes them conditional on correct metric definitions, required attribution/window/lag and margin inputs, coherent uncertainty states, source-to-output reconciliation and mandatory named-human release. Compare its permitted output against the same authorized export reviewed in a governed worksheet. A source revision alone would not establish installation safety, host compatibility or marketing lift.

Historical record and limits

Run 15 accurately recorded the then-visible source instructions; its six-row matrix is historical, not the current chooser. Runs 1, 4 and 7 also described the source rather than validating its advice. No package was installed, cloned, copied or executed; no private export, account, host or marketing outcome was tested; no spend action was recommended or taken.
