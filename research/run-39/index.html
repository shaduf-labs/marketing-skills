Research report · 31 October 2026

One owned URL: separate AI-search evidence from page checks

Use a small, owner-maintained evidence sheet for one authorized public URL. A page audit describes the page; platform reports describe particular appearances; analytics describes visits. None of these alone predicts whether a page will be cited after an edit.

Decision

Keep the unscored page checklist for page diagnostics and add a separate evidence sheet for visibility observations. No AI-readiness score, fifth skill card or installation follows. The four active cards remain conditional; Search Page Audit and Ad Campaign Analyzer remain historical nonrecommendations.

Five evidence lanes, not one metric

LaneWhat an authorized owner can recordWhat it does not show
Page and indexing checksDated fetch/rendered observations, canonical and robots controls, and a verified-property Google URL Inspection result.Visibility or citation. A successful live test does not guarantee indexing or selection as canonical.
Google Search performanceOrdinary Search Console Performance page-filtered clicks and impressions for a dated range.An AI-only click or citation count. AI feature traffic is included in Web totals; page/query filters and anonymized queries affect totals.
Google generative SearchThe dedicated generative AI performance (Search) report shows page-level AI Overview/AI Mode impressions, where available. It rolled out worldwide by 31 August 2026.A citation ledger, clicks, rank, answer text or all-platform reach. Low activity may hide the report; newest data can change, and export converts unavailable ~/- values to zero. Canonical assignment and chart/table totals require care.
Bing-supported AI answersBing Webmaster Tools AI Performance Pages view reports page-level visible citation counts and grouped grounding phrases for supported surfaces. The public-preview announcement confirms URL-level citations.Every AI answer, exact prompts, rank or clicks. The report is aggregated/sampled, and sparse activity may be absent.
Visits and sampled answersWith permission and instrumentation, a GA4 landing page by session source/medium is a visit-referral proxy. A manually saved answer is one dated, contextual sighting.No-click appearances or citation frequency. Direct/none may hide origin; Clarity notes Google AI Overview and ordinary Search share the google.com referrer. One screenshot is not stable rank.

Minimum sheet for one URL

Record the authorized URL and its canonical mapping; page purpose and question; platform and report name; date range and timezone; URL filter and metric definition; source export or screenshot; value only when supplied; preliminary, sampling or low-volume notes; and a named SEO/site reviewer. Keep page diagnostics, Google impressions, Bing citations, search clicks, analytics visits and answer sightings in separate rows. Mark missing access unknown, not zero. Preserve Google's displayed ~/- state before export.

The site owner reviews evidence and any proposed change. Content or technical edits need separate approval and a separate measurement design. Do not request credentials, protected URLs or new tracking simply to fill the sheet. A before/after count or screenshot alone cannot attribute a change to a page edit.

What remains unknown

This is a source-and-method review, not a measurement for a property. No owned URL, report export, analytics view or sampled answer was examined. Report availability, canonical mapping, values, visit attribution and any outcome remain unknown. Google's AI-feature guidance says eligibility does not guarantee serving and links can vary. Google impressions and Bing citations use different definitions and cannot be added into a universal visibility rate.

A portable skill would be worth comparing only after repeated authorized cases expose material omissions or reviewer burden in the maintained sheet. A platform-report change by itself calls for a sheet update, not a package search.
