Research report · 27 October 2026

One approved article → two social drafts

Core Haines Social Content describes turning a blog insight into LinkedIn copy and takeaways into an X thread. That supports a narrow drafting hypothesis, not a fifth catalogue card or a posting recommendation.

Decision

Start with a maintained article-to-post template and reviewed prompt. Keep Social Content as a research lead until its exact payload, host permissions and added value over that simpler route can be checked. The four conditional cards and two historical nonrecommendations do not change.

The bounded job

Given one already-approved, non-sensitive article, produce one LinkedIn text draft and one short X thread draft as local, unscheduled text. The brief would need the article's approved facts, canonical URL, attribution and rights, intended reader, voice and exact CTA. None was supplied for this review. Each material claim should point to an article passage or be marked unresolved. A named reviewer checks factual fidelity, quotations and rights, voice, URL and CTA, then separately decides whether to publish.

The claim pointers and local release gate are proposed review controls, not requirements found in the skill source. “Short” is a proposed output constraint: the source templates show roughly seven- or eight-post X examples, not a short-thread rule.

What would change this decision?

A stable selected revision, complete same-skill payload and chosen-host permission surface would be needed before a controlled comparison. With an authorized article, compare the template and skill route using the same brief and fixed rubric: traceable claims, quotation/rights and CTA errors, unauthorized reads or actions, reviewer effort and useful draft quality. If the template matches or beats the skill without material omissions, the added-value hypothesis fails.

The broader source also contains calendar, analytics, video and optimization claims. They were not adopted or outcome-tested for this text-draft decision.

Provenance and limits

The mutable main selected file displayed name: social and version 2.4.0 in a web-broker rendering on 27 October. It and four linked same-skill references were read. “Crawled: today” and “Crawled: 2 days ago” were broker cache labels, not proof of fresh origin content. A cached path-history page did not align with the displayed file; API access and container Git resolution failed. No selected-file SHA/date, immutable intended revision, full package inventory, installed payload or effective host behavior was established.

No article, account, analytics, synthetic case, skill invocation, scheduling, posting or marketing outcome was used or observed. Source links describe instructions and claims, not a verified result.
