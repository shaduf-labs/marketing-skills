Research report · 18 October 2026

What changed in the tagged emails instructions?

A complete selected-file comparison of 2.1.0 at v2.11.8 and 2.1.4 at v2.11.37 finds four changed areas: an outbound referral, reference-before-drafting routing, use of local Learnings, and implementation links rewritten to mutable main URLs. The bounded local-draft choice does not become a send plan.

Reader decision

For a non-personal welcome draft, start with an owner-maintained template and reviewed prompt. Our five-email Markdown exercise is a proposed scope, not the skill’s required length. If the skill is later considered, authorize every context and reference read separately; do not treat its instructions as permission to open a local product file. A named reviewer checks proof, terms and CTA destinations. Recipient-policy and ESP owners separately approve any import, setup, test, schedule or send.

The comparison boundary

Both tagged skills/emails/SKILL.md files were returned in full: 330 numbered lines plus a trailing newline at 2.1.0, and 342 plus a trailing newline at 2.1.4. Their Git blob SHA-1 identifiers are 6afcc8894a928653d7e7f8a0b8bbef871e08e32e and e660baf4adf7430ff90bfbdeaf9a9ad101a2d2ed, respectively. These identify selected source files, not a release ZIP or installed package. The 2.1.0 file-attributable commit and later tag-target commit 365b412152b9542499ca1439426f7fc3cc92bb65 are separate provenance points.

What to do with a local welcome brief

  1. Use the simpler first routeWrite an approved, non-personal brief in an owner-maintained template and try a reviewed prompt. A five-email Markdown outline is a review exercise, not a source requirement.
  2. Scope any later skill trialReview the intended package and host first. Approve exactly which local context, Learnings and reference files may be read. Keep unsupported claims and missing performance facts visibly unresolved.
  3. Keep action separateHave a named person check proof, terms and exact CTA URLs. Recipient-policy and ESP owners decide eligibility, consent, suppression, overlap, timing and every external email-system action.

A direct template-versus-skill comparison still needs a consenting, bounded brief, a defined rubric and named reviewers. Neither route was tested here.

Limits: This is the net 2.1.0→2.1.4 selected-file change, not an exact 2.1.3→2.1.4 diff. The 2.1.3 reading was a dated mutable observation, not a complete saved immutable endpoint. Tagged text does not prove today’s main, ZIP entries or manifest, installation behavior, linked-file resolution, privacy/legal safety, deliverability, draft quality or efficacy. No customer context, account, credential, ESP, recipient data, draft or send was used.
