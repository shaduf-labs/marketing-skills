Marketing Skills Catalog

Choose a marketing skill by the job and review required.

Six source-inspected instruction packages for marketers using AI agents, including one conditional lifecycle-email draft candidate. Start with a concrete output, inspect the original source, and keep consequential decisions with a person.

6source-inspected skills in this low-access set; one is a conditional email-draft candidate
0independently tested installs, outcome tests, or security audits
1rule for all entries: human approval before publishing, sending, deploying, or changing spend

The first set produces drafts, plans, research, audits, and analysis.

These are portable skills with a SKILL.md instruction package, not automation platforms or account connectors. The visual map shows the output each package is designed to produce—not a claimed result.

Six skills to assess

Every item below is source-inspected; not independently tested. “Compatible” refers to the maintainer’s statement, not this catalogue’s install test.

01 · Draft

Copywriting

Draft or improve homepage, landing-page, pricing-page, feature-page, about-page, or product-page copy.

Output: a page-copy draft based on audience, offer, proof, and traffic context.

  • Content
  • Draft only
  • MIT
  • Version 2.0.2
REVIEW BEFORE USECheck every claim, testimonial, brand fit, accessibility and legal requirement before publication.
02 · Plan

Content Strategy

Turn business context into content pillars, topic clusters, buyer-stage mapping, priorities, gaps, and a first-month editorial calendar.

Output: a planning package—not published content.

  • Content planning
  • Internet for research
  • MIT
  • Version 1.0
REVIEW BEFORE USEApprove the strategy; validate competitive and keyword evidence; handle customer material with authorization.
03 · Research

Competitor Discovery

Build a dated, classified public-web list of direct, adjacent, and tangential competitors.

Output: names, URLs, types, one-liners, queries used, and next steps.

  • Competitive research
  • Internet required
  • MIT
  • Version 1.0
REVIEW BEFORE USEConfirm the list before using it. Recheck time-sensitive facts before a public comparison or sales claim.
04 · Audit

Search Page Audit

Audit a supplied URL across SEO basics, content structure, AI-search/GEO readiness, and E-E-A-T signals.

Output: a 38-point scored checklist and prioritized fixes—not site deployment.

  • Search content
  • Public URL fetch
  • MIT
  • Version 1.0
REVIEW BEFORE USEDo not submit protected URLs without authorization. Validate audit findings and crawler-policy interpretation before deployment.
05 · Analyze

Ad Campaign Analyzer

Read a CSV, table, or pasted export to frame CPA, ROAS, LTV:CAC, creative fatigue, and stop/hold/scale choices.

Output: recommendations and a proposed scaling plan—not an ad-account action.

  • Paid media
  • Sensitive exports
  • MIT
  • Version 1.0
REVIEW BEFORE USECheck attribution, margins, LTV assumptions, statistical adequacy, and learning constraints before changing a budget.
06 · Draft

emails

Conditionally draft a lifecycle-email sequence from an owner-approved, non-personal brief.

Output: a five-email welcome-sequence brief in Markdown—not an email-program configuration or send.

  • Lifecycle/email
  • Conditional candidate
  • Draft only
  • SHA/date not established
  • MIT stated
  • Version 2.0.0
OWNER GATEDo not load local product context without authorization or follow linked tools. A person separately approves every import, configuration, schedule, test, publication, or send.
Excluded from this low-access set: RampStack email-sequences. Its inspected workflow directs email-service-provider configuration, a self-send, broadcast scheduling, and measurement. Read the lifecycle/email candidate review.

Start with a bounded job.

  1. Name the outputDraft copy, a plan, a lifecycle-email brief, a research table, an audit, or an analysis.
  2. Read the sourceCheck the original file, license, install guidance, and any included resources.
  3. Scope the dataUse only authorized material. Treat campaign exports and customer context as sensitive.
  4. Approve the actionVerify facts and assumptions before a public or consequential change.
